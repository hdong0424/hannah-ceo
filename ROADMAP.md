# Roadmap

This roadmap records the current direction. It can change as Hannah learns, tests ideas, and makes new decisions.

**Current focus:** [Vlog IP Project](projects/vlog-ip/README.md)

## Three-month objectives

1. Establish a clear, maintainable public journal for entrepreneurship and coding work.
2. Rebuild practical development confidence through small, explainable changes.
3. Explore several possible AI business problems without committing too early.
4. Select one narrow problem using evidence from potential users or customers.
5. Build a small AI prototype whose purpose and implementation Hannah can explain.

## Working sequence

### 1. Organize the record

Create a simple structure for venture notes, projects, coding lessons, and decisions.

### 2. Document the starting point

Record current objectives, known experience, open questions, and the first small action.

### 3. Rebuild through practice

Complete small technical exercises and project improvements while recording what changed and why.

### 4. Explore AI opportunities

Compare business problems, intended users, existing alternatives, and evidence of real demand.

### 5. Build one focused prototype

Implement the smallest useful version, test it with representative users, and document the result.

## Not decided yet

- The first AI problem or industry
- The service or product business model
- The application framework and hosting platform
- The public brand, visual identity, and domain
