# Vlog IP Project

**Status:** Exploring  
**Started:** 2026-08-16  
**Primary language:** Chinese

## Project purpose

Build and improve a vlog-based personal IP project as a real business experiment.

The initial purpose combines several possible outcomes:

- Build professional authority and credibility
- Attract potential customers for a future business or agency
- Develop an audience that may support future products or services
- Publicly document entrepreneurship and coding progress
- Explore Hannah's direction before selecting one business model

These are starting hypotheses, not proven outcomes. The project will use published work and audience response to learn which direction deserves greater focus.

## Initial audience

The primary intended audience is entrepreneurs and small-business owners.

The secondary audience will remain broad at first. Hannah will observe who responds, what questions they ask, and which subjects create useful conversations before narrowing the audience.

## Content approach

The first content will be mainly in Chinese. Topics, formats, platforms, and publishing frequency have not yet been selected.

## Version-one objective

Create a small, repeatable publishing experiment that Hannah can sustain long enough to learn from real audience response.

Version one should answer:

1. Which problems or experiences can Hannah discuss with useful specificity?
2. Which content attracts the intended audience?
3. What type of response indicates genuine interest rather than passive views?
4. Which business opportunity, if any, begins to appear through repeated feedback?

## Evidence to record

- What was published and where
- The hypothesis behind each piece
- Questions, replies, and conversations it generated
- Qualitative patterns in audience response
- Public-safe performance measurements
- What Hannah learned and what she will change next

Do not record private messages, customer information, account credentials, unpublished financial details, or another person's identifying information without permission.

## Open decisions

- The first content theme
- The initial social platform
- The publishing format and frequency
- The first business hypothesis to test
- The measurements that will guide the next decision

## Next step

Choose one initial content theme and define a small first publishing experiment.
